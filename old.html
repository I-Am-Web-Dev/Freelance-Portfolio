About Atlas Café

Established in 2018, Atlas Café has been serving the local community with fresh coffee, pastries and simple homemade food. We believe in good products, friendly service and a comfortable atmosphere.

Our café is open every day and welcomes families, students, professionals and anyone looking for a quiet place to enjoy a coffee.
